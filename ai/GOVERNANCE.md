# U-GAS Governance

U-GAS is a portable workflow model, not authority over a repository. Current repository files, explicit owner intent, and verified remote state outrank conversation memory or copied policy.

Before acting, identify the target, purpose, branch, local changes, and source of truth. Preserve uncertainty as `UNKNOWN` or `PURPOSE UNKNOWN` rather than inventing an answer. Separate these claims: source was read, runtime was ready, a test executed, and a test passed.

Routine reversible work may be automated. Human approval remains appropriate for authentication, secrets, destructive or irreversible operations, legal/security choices, physical devices, and meaningful product decisions.
