# U-GAS Session Continuity

`CURRENT_STATE.md` is the compact resume authority. `PROGRESS.md` is chronological history and evidence. `IDEAS.md` is unaccepted possibility space. Do not let the newest history entry silently override an explicit current state.

For resume work, follow:

`RESUME ACTION -> RECONCILE -> VERIFY -> PROJECT NEXT`

Record a checkpoint when a material decision, blocker, pause, handoff, or completed milestone would otherwise be lost. Preserve only what changed, the evidence/source, what remains pending, and the next concrete safe action. Never claim continuity from model memory alone.
